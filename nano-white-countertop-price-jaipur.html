Material Selection Guide

Nano White vs Specta Quartz vs Granite

Nano White G5

The best option for homeowners seeking a 100 percent snow-white countertop without resin binders. Completely impervious to haldi, lemon, tea, and heated pans. Requires experienced fabricators with bridge saws.

  • Zero porosity (0.00% water absorption)
  • Heat tolerance up to 300°C
  • Purest optical white surface

Specta Quartz

Engineered quartz made with 93 percent crushed natural quartz and polymer resins. Offers rich marble veining (Gold Rush, Venera), seamless waterfall edges, and flexible fabricability.

  • Designer Italian marble patterns
  • High impact resistance (no chip edges)
  • Official 10-year manufacturer warranty

Rajasthan Granite

Natural volcanic gangsaw stone (Black Galaxy, Steel Grey, Lakha Red). Highly scratch-resistant, heavy-duty, and economical for heavy daily Indian cooking.

  • Economical (Rs. 65 - Rs. 240/sq.ft.)
  • Direct quarry extraction in Rajasthan
  • Virtually indestructible
