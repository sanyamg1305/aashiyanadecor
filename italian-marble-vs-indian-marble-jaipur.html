Choosing between Imported Italian Marble and Indian Makrana Marble defines your villa aesthetic and long-term maintenance. Italian marble provides dramatic bookmatched diamond veining and high-luster European opulence. Marble FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for flooring in Jaipur: Italian Marble or Indian Marble?

Both stones excel in different spaces. Italian marble (Statuario, Botticino, Calacatta) is superior for formal living rooms and lobbies because of dramatic bookmatched diamond veining and high crystalline luster. Indian Makrana marble is superior for bedrooms and pooja mandirs because it has 98 percent calcium carbonate purity, never turns yellow over 50 years, and has higher natural density.

How do Italian marble and Indian marble compare in pricing in Jaipur?

Indian Makrana marble (Kumari, Dungri, and Albeta white grades) is generally more accessible across residential budgets, while imported Italian marble (Carrara, Botticino, Statuario Extra) sits in a higher luxury tier depending on origin and vein rarity. Does Italian marble require more maintenance than Indian marble?

Yes. Italian marble is slightly softer (Mohs hardness 3 to 3.5) and more porous (0.3 to 0.5% water absorption) compared to Makrana marble. It requires epoxy net backing and periodic penetrating sealers to prevent citrus and turmeric stains, and re-polishing every 7 to 10 years.
