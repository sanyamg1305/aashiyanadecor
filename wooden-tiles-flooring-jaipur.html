Climate & Practicality

Why Wooden Vitrified Tiles Outperform Real Wood in Jaipur

1. 100% Termite & Borer Proof: Rajasthan homes regularly face termite attacks that destroy solid wood and MDF baseboards within years. Vitrified porcelain is fully inorganic and impervious to insect damage.

2. Zero Water Absorption: Unlike real timber that swells, buckles, or warps when wet, vitrified tiles absorb less than 0.05 percent water. You can mop with water, clean with disinfectants, and install them directly in wet areas.

3. Thermal Comfort & Durability: In Jaipur temperatures exceeding 45 degrees Celsius, real wood dries out and splits. Vitrified tiles retain natural cooling properties underfoot and never need sanding or varnishing.
