Architect & Builder FAQs

How do Jaipur architects book swatch boxes or dry-lay floor appointments?

Design studios can message our trade desk on WhatsApp (+91 94686 34597) with studio details and project BOQ requirements to request couriered swatch kits across Jaipur or book an illuminated dry-lay floor walkthrough at our Gopalpura Bypass warehouse.

Can full Italian marble blocks and tile batches be reserved across multi-month construction schedules?

Yes. Once an architect or builder confirms a numbered lot or porcelain batch at our Mansarovar stockyard, we tag and hold the dedicated pallets or sequential gangsaw slabs so phased villa deliveries arrive with zero shade variation.
