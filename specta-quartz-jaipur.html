Material Science

Specta Quartz vs Natural Granite for Indian Kitchens

Turmeric & Oil Resistance

Specta Quartz: 100% non-porous. Haldi, ghee, and mustard oil never penetrate.

Granite: Porous natural stone that requires periodic chemical sealing to prevent dark oil spots.

Color & Vein Symmetry

Specta Quartz: Predictable, bookmatched Calacatta and gold veining across multiple slabs.

Granite: Random geological variation that can make corner joints look misaligned.

Warranty & Food Safety

Specta Quartz: NSF-certified food-safe surface with an official manufacturer warranty.

Granite: Natural stone sold without formal stain warranty.
