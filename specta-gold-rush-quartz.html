Why Gold Rush Replaces Natural Calacatta in Indian Kitchens

Natural white marble etches rapidly when exposed to lemon juice, vinegar, or hot tadka splatters. Formulated using over 90% natural quartz crystals bound with Bretonstone-grade polymer resins, Specta Gold Rush captures warm golden-amber Calacatta veining on an ultra-clean white body without porosity. As an authorized Specta dealer at Gopalpura Bypass, Mansarovar, Aashiyana Decor supplies factory-authenticated slabs backed by a 10-year manufacturer warranty.

Zero-Sealing Hygiene

With water absorption below 0.02%, Gold Rush never requires annual penetrating sealers. Turmeric, coffee, tamarind, and cooking oils stay on the surface and wipe away cleanly with mild soap water—review our countertop maintenance guide for daily care tips.

Waterfall Islands & Vanity Tops
