Indian Kitchen Stress Test: Granite vs. Quartz vs. Nano White

Indian culinary preparation involves four elements that destroy inferior countertops: high turmeric content, hot brass utensils, acidic lemon and tomato gravies, and heavy pestle pounding. Here is how the top materials perform:

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for heavy Indian cooking: Granite or Quartz?

Natural granite (such as Black Galaxy or Steel Grey) is best if you place hot cookware directly on the slab without trivets. Specta Quartz is superior if you want bright marble veining and zero turmeric stain absorption without annual sealing.

Why is Nano White glass susceptible to thermal shock?

Nano White is a crystallized glass product. Rapid temperature changes from hot pans or heavy pressure cooker weight can cause sudden hairline fractures that cannot be repaired, unlike natural granite or engineered quartz.
