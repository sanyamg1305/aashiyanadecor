Why Indian Cooking Demands Specta Quartz Over Natural Marble

Natural Italian marble, while luxurious, consists primarily of calcium carbonate (calcite). In Indian kitchens where curries, tamarind, lemon juice, and haldi are used daily, marble reacts instantly to acids, leaving dull etched spots and yellow turmeric stains that penetrate deep into the stone pores.

Specta Quartz solves this permanently through high-pressure vacuum vibro-compression:

  • Zero Porosity (<0.03% water absorption): Turmeric stains wipe clean with water and mild dish soap even after sitting overnight.
  • Mohs Hardness 7: Knife cuts and chef preparation will not scratch the surface.
  • Standard Jumbo Slabs (3200 x 1600 mm): Allows large L-shaped countertops and 10-foot waterfall islands with seamless joints.

What is the price of Specta Quartz in Jaipur per sq ft?
