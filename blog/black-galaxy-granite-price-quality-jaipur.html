Black Galaxy Granite: Price, Grading, Golden Flecks & Buying Guide in Jaipur

Globally revered by luxury architects as Star Galaxy, Black Galaxy Granite is mined exclusively in Chimakurthy near Ongole in Andhra Pradesh, India. Renowned for its jet-black basalt base scattered with shimmering golden, copper, and silver crystals of bronzite (enstatite), this stone creates an unmistakable celestial look in any architectural project.

1. The Science of Golden Bronzite Flecks

What differentiates genuine Black Galaxy from ordinary black granite is the presence of metallic bronzite crystals embedded during volcanic magma cooling millions of years ago. Under ambient sunlight or warm spotlighting, these crystals reflect light at varied angles, sparkling like gold dust floating in the night sky.

2. How Black Galaxy is Graded

At our stockyard at Gopalpura Bypass, Mansarovar, we educate clients that Black Galaxy is categorized into three commercial grades based on crystal distribution:

  • Grade A (Premium Export Quality): Jet-black pitch-dark background with dense, evenly distributed medium-sized golden flecks. Zero white streaks (dry lines), zero black patches, and zero epoxy filling.
  • Grade B (Standard Commercial): Solid black background with slightly irregular fleck sizes (mix of small and medium specks). May exhibit faint lines that are typically placed near cut edges during masonry.
  • Grade C (Economy Lots): Dark grey-black background with sporadic flecks, occasional quartz veins, and noticeable color variation across slabs.
Small vs Medium vs Big Fleck Galaxy Fleck size is a matter of aesthetic preference. Small Fleck Galaxy offers a subdued, star-mist look favored for sleek kitchen countertops. Medium and Large Fleck Galaxy delivers a dramatic, bold sparkle that looks breathtaking on staircases and TV console feature walls.

3. How to Spot Artificially Dyed Fakes in Jaipur

Due to Black Galaxy's high market demand, some unscrupulous local traders dye cheaper grey granites with chemical black epoxy dyes to pass them off as premium Galaxy. Here is our 3-step showroom test to verify authenticity:

  1. The Acetone Test: Rub a white cotton cloth dipped in acetone or petrol on the slab surface. If the cloth turns black or yellow, the slab has been artificially dyed. Genuine Black Galaxy will leave the cloth clean.
  2. Examine the Edge Cross-Section: Look at the unpolished side of the slab. Natural Black Galaxy is dark charcoal grey throughout its core. If only the top 1mm is black while the core is light grey, it is chemically treated.
  3. Inspect Fleck Uniformity: Natural bronzite crystals are embedded deep inside the stone matrix and exhibit genuine 3D optical depth when you tilt your head. Printed or coated imitations look flat and superficial.

4. Top Applications: Stairs, Kitchens & TV Units

Because Black Galaxy has high compressive strength and near-zero porosity, it excels in diverse residential and commercial spaces:

  • Staircase Treads & Risers: A Black Galaxy tread with a double full-bullnose edge and 3-groove brass safety inlay is one of the most durable, regal staircase designs for Jaipur duplexes.
  • Kitchen Countertops: Impervious to turmeric, boiling oils, and water stains when sealed.
  • Bathroom Vanity Tops: Complements brass gold faucets and vessel basins effortlessly.
  • TV Feature Walls & Fireplaces: Polished bookmatched vertical cladding that reflects living room chandeliers.
