Countertop & Staircase Edge Profiles

Modern Luxury Trend

40mm Mitered Apron Edge

Two pieces cut at 45-degree angles joined with color-matched epoxy to create the visual illusion of a thick, heavy monolithic 40mm slab. Ideal for kitchen islands and Specta Quartz waterfall drops.

Best for Staircases

Full Bullnose (Round Profile)

Fully semicircular rounded edge along the entire slab thickness. Eliminates sharp corners, prevents edge chipping, and provides comfortable footing for staircase steps and risers.

Half Bullnose (Demi Bullnose)

Edge Moulding FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular countertop edge profile for modular kitchens in Jaipur?

For modern modular kitchens in Jaipur, the 40mm mitered apron edge (sandwich waterfall joint) is the most requested, giving slabs a solid thick monolithic stone presence. For busy family kitchens prioritizing zero sharp edges, a half bullnose or pencil edge is recommended.

How is edge moulding charged for granite and quartz in Jaipur?

Edge moulding is typically calculated per running foot based on profile complexity (basic pencil and chamfer bevel edges, full and half bullnose, or premium 40mm mitered joints and classical Ogee edges) plus slab material and wet diamond polishing. Which edge profile is recommended for staircase steps?

Full bullnose is the gold standard for staircase steps and risers. The rounded edge prevents chipped nosing from foot traffic and reduces injury risk, especially when paired with 2 or 3 anti-skid groove cuts along the front tread.

Can quartz countertop edges be polished on-site?

Yes, but engineered quartz requires wet diamond polishing pads up to 3000 grit. Dry grinding burns the polymer resins and discolors white quartz. We recommend factory water-jet edge profiling or experienced stone fabricators for Specta Quartz.
