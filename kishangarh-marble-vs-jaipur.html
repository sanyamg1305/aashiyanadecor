Showroom Advantage

Why Jaipur Chooses Aashiyana Decor

01

Dry-Lay Bookmatch Floor

Never purchase imported marble from a vertical rack alone. At our Gopalpura Bypass yard, we dry-lay slabs side-by-side on our warehouse floor so you can inspect natural vein flow, bookmatching, and lighting under genuine daylight conditions.

02

Zero Transit Damage Risk

Italian marble and white Statuario are naturally brittle. When hauled 100km on rough highway stretches, hairline micro-fissures often turn into broken corners. We absorb all transit risks - every slab is delivered intact to your Jaipur doorstep.

03

Direct Authorized Dealerships

Beyond natural marble and Rajasthan gangsaw granite, Aashiyana Decor is the authorized dealer for Specta Quartz in Jaipur. One destination covers luxury Italian marble, stain-free kitchen countertops, and vitrified floor tiles.

04

7-Point Slab Verification

Every bundle undergoes digital caliper thickness measurement, fiberglass mesh inspection, daylight dry-lay bookmatching, and single-block lot segregation. You get documented quality assurance before stone is dispatched to your site.
