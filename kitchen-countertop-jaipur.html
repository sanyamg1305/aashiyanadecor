Countertop FAQs

What is the best stone for an Indian kitchen countertop in Jaipur?

The two best choices are Specta Engineered Quartz and Gangsaw Granite. Specta Quartz is ideal for modern kitchens wanting white or light marble aesthetics because it is non-porous and guaranteed against turmeric (haldi) stains. Gangsaw Granite (like Black Galaxy or Steel Grey) is ideal if you want rock-solid heat resistance up to 600 degrees Celsius to place boiling pans directly on the slab.

How are kitchen countertop stones priced in Jaipur?

Kitchen countertop slab pricing in Jaipur depends on the material (gangsaw granite such as Steel Grey or Black Galaxy, authorized Specta Quartz, or G5/G7 Nano White glass), slab thickness (15mm, 18mm, or 20mm), and edge profile. Can hot utensils be placed directly on Quartz countertops?

Specta Quartz withstands short-term thermal exposure up to 150 degrees Celsius. However, placing a red-hot pressure cooker or tawa directly from flame onto quartz can cause thermal shock to the polymer binding resins. For quartz, we always recommend using silicone trivets or steel hot pads.

What edge profile is recommended for modern modular kitchens?

For modern kitchens, the 40mm mitered apron (sandwich joint) and 45-degree chamfer edge are the most popular, giving slabs a solid thick monolithic look. For high-traffic family homes with children, a half-bullnose or pencil edge is recommended to prevent chipped edges.
