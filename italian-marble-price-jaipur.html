How to Choose Italian Marble in Jaipur

Italian marble is renowned for its crystalline purity and feathery veining. When evaluating slabs at our Mansarovar stockyard, our master stone graders assist you across 4 essential checks:

  • 01 / Vein Continuity & Bookmatching: Every consecutive slab from the block is mapped to create mirrored symmetrical diamonds.
  • 02 / Calibrated Thickness: Ensuring uniform 18mm thickness across all corners to eliminate undulation during laying.
  • 03 / Natural Resin Backing: High-tensile European mesh applied with food-grade epoxy ensures zero cracking during transportation.
  • 04 / Surface Polishing: Choices between High-Gloss Mirror Polish, Honed Matte, or Tactile Leather finish.
