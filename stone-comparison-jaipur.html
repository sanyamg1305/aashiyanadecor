Industry Quality Standard

The Aashiyana 7-Point Slab Verification Protocol

To guarantee architectural perfection for luxury homes across Jaipur, every lot of imported marble, gangsaw granite, and Specta Quartz at our Gopalpura Bypass stockyard undergoes our mandatory 7-stage quality audit before dispatch.

1

Digital Caliper Thickness Test

We measure slab edges across 6 points with digital micrometers to verify uniform 18mm or 20mm gangsaw calibration. This prevents edge stepping and lippage during installation.

2

Acid & Porosity Drop Inspection

Sample offcuts undergo citrus and oil drop tests to measure natural absorption rates. This determines whether high-penetration resin sealers are required before flooring installation.

3

Fiber Mesh & Epoxy Reinforcement

All imported Italian marble slabs are audited for high-tensile fiberglass net backing and vacuum epoxy infiltration. This protects delicate crystalline veins during transit and cutting.

4

Natural Daylight Dry-Lay Audit

We dry-lay consecutive slabs across our stockyard floor under true ambient sunlight. Clients and architects can verify vein direction, color tone consistency, and bookmatched symmetry.

5

Mohs Hardness Scratch Verification

Specta Quartz and granite surfaces are tested against Mohs hardness picks (scale 6 to 7) to confirm scratch immunity against kitchen knives, utensils, and footwear traffic.

6

Diagonal Square-Cut Alignment

We check orthogonal diagonal dimensions on large format slabs to ensure accurate 90-degree corners. This cuts down on-site stone trimming losses from 15 percent down to under 8 percent.

7

Single-Block Extraction Batch Segregation

Every delivery package is assembled exclusively from consecutive cuts of the same quarry block. This prevents the common problem of mismatched tones between living room and hallway flooring.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for Indian kitchen countertops: Quartz or Granite?

Both are top performers with distinct benefits. Specta Quartz provides superior stain resistance against turmeric, mustard oil, and lemon juice because of its non-porous structure, along with contemporary white and Calacatta veining. Gangsaw Granite provides extreme direct heat resistance (up to 600 degrees Celsius), allowing hot pans directly on the surface without trivets. If you want zero stain anxiety and modern marble look, choose Quartz. If you place boiling utensils directly, choose Granite.

What is the difference between Italian Marble and Vitrified Tiles?

Italian marble is a 100 percent natural quarried stone cut into full gangsaw slabs. It provides grand seamless bookmatched diamonds and can be repolished every 8 to 10 years to restore its pristine mirror finish. Vitrified tiles are factory-made ceramic slabs with printed patterns; they require zero sealing and cost significantly less (Rs 45 to 120/sq.ft. vs Rs 450 to 1,800/sq.ft.), but feature joint lines and cannot be repolished.

Does Specta Quartz absorb turmeric or masala stains?

No. Specta Quartz has a certified water absorption rate below 0.02 percent, making it practically non-porous. Unlike natural marble or unsealed granite, Specta Quartz does not absorb turmeric, tea, coffee, wine, or oil. Spills wipe clean easily with mild liquid soap.

What is Nano White and how does it compare to Quartz?

Nano White (G5/G7) is a crystallized glass composite that is 100 percent non-porous and acid-resistant, providing an ultra-pure clinical white look without grains. However, glass is brittle and requires expert diamond fabrication during sink cutouts to prevent edge chipping. Specta Quartz incorporates polymer resin flexibility, making edge miter joints and sink cutouts cleaner and less prone to edge chipping.
