Specta Quartz & Engineered Surfaces

Experience the pinnacle of culinary surfaces. Engineered quartz delivers unmatched stain resistance, zero porosity, and seamless aesthetics without the maintenance of natural marble.

Non-Porous

Will not absorb oils, turmeric, coffee or citrus liquids. 100% stain proof.

Anti-Bacterial

Food-grade certified surface that prevents microbial and mould buildup.

Heat & Scratch Proof

High Mohs hardness ensures durability against pots, pans, and chef knives.

Zero Sealing Needed

Unlike natural marble, quartz never requires chemical polishing or annual sealing.
