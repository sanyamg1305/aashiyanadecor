Engineering & Detailing

Factory Edge Moulding & Anti-Skid Detailing

1. Full Bullnose Edge

Completely rounded profile on tread front. Safe for children, comfortable under bare feet, and completely prevents edge chipped corners.

2. Anti-Skid Grooves (2-Line/3-Line)

Precision CNC grooves cut 1 inch away from tread nose. Traps footwear soles to provide non-skid friction on high-gloss polish stairs.

3. Double-Sandwich Miter Landing

Creates a grand 40mm thick fascia for mid-landing platforms and first entrance steps, giving stairways an imposing architectural presence.

Staircase Granite FAQs

What is the price of staircase granite in Jaipur?

Staircase granite in Jaipur ranges from Rs 95 to 145 per sq.ft. for Steel Grey or Rajasthan Black granite, Rs 160 to 240 per sq.ft. for premium Black Galaxy, and Rs 120 to 180 per sq.ft. for Tan Brown. Factory edge moulding (full bullnose) ranges between Rs 40 and 70 per running foot.

What are standard dimensions for granite steps and risers?

Standard steps (treads) are 3 to 6 feet in length, 11 to 12 inches in width, and 18mm to 20mm in thickness. Risers are typically 3 to 6 feet in length and 6 to 7 inches in height. Pre-cut ready lots reduce site cutting waste to under 3 percent.

How to make polished granite stairs slip-resistant?

To prevent slipping on internal or external stairs, Aashiyana Decor provides three proven options: (1) 2-line or 3-line diamond grooves cut 1 inch away from the bullnose edge, (2) flamed or shot-blasted textured treads, or (3) inlaid brass anti-skid strips.

Which edge profile is best for staircase steps?

The Full Bullnose (completely rounded edge) is the best profile for staircase steps because it eliminates sharp 90-degree corners, protects children and senior citizens from painful shin bruises, and resists edge chipping over decades.
