Elevation FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best stone for exterior front elevation in Jaipur?

Flamed granite (such as Steel Grey or Black Pearl) and natural Rajasthani sandstone (Mint, Teakwood, Dholpur) are the best choices for Jaipur front elevations. Unlike exterior paint or ceramic tiles that peel or crack under 45 degree Celsius summer heat, flamed granite and natural sandstone are 100 percent UV proof and weather resistant for decades.

What is the price of exterior stone cladding in Jaipur?

Exterior wall cladding in Jaipur ranges from Rs 55 to Rs 95 per sq.ft. for 300x600mm vitrified elevation tiles, Rs 85 to Rs 160 per sq.ft. for flamed and leather finish granite slabs, and Rs 120 to Rs 240 per sq.ft. for grooved natural sandstone at Aashiyana Decor stockyard.

What is mechanical dry cladding and when is it required?

Mechanical dry cladding uses stainless steel brackets and anchors to fix stone slabs to exterior walls with an air gap, eliminating mortar. For heights above 12 feet or slabs heavier than 20kg, dry cladding is mandatory for building safety to prevent detachment during thermal expansion.

Does flamed granite absorb rainwater during Jaipur monsoons?

No. Granite has a natural water absorption rate below 0.4 percent. The high-temperature thermal flame treatment roughens the surface for texture while preserving the stone structural density, preventing dampness or efflorescence.
