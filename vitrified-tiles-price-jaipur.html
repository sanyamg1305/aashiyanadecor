Frequently Asked Questions

How are vitrified tiles priced per sq.ft. in Jaipur?

Vitrified tile pricing in Jaipur depends on format (600x600mm double charge, 600x1200mm GVT/PGVT, or large-format 800x1600mm porcelain slabs), glaze finish, and order volume. Visit Aashiyana Decor on Gopalpura Bypass or message +91 94686 34597 on WhatsApp for wholesale stockyard quotes.

What is the difference between GVT and PGVT tiles?

GVT (Glazed Vitrified Tiles) feature a glazed surface available in matte, rustic, satin, or carving textures with high slip resistance. PGVT (Polished Glazed Vitrified Tiles) receive an additional high-gloss nano polish creating a mirror-like reflective finish ideal for living rooms and formal halls.

Are double charge vitrified tiles better for high-traffic floors?

Yes. Double charge tiles are manufactured by pressing two layers of pigment, resulting in a 3mm to 4mm thick wear layer. This makes them significantly more resistant to surface abrasion and fading in high-traffic spaces like living halls, retail stores, and commercial offices.

How do vitrified tile prices in Jaipur compare to buying directly from Morbi?

While base ex-factory prices in Morbi may appear lower, factoring in Gujarat to Rajasthan freight, GST, transit breakage risk, and large minimum box ordering constraints makes buying directly from Aashiyana Decor stockyard in Jaipur more economical with same-day batch replacement.
