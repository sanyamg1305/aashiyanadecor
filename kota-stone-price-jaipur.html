Kota Stone FAQs

How is Kota stone priced in Jaipur?

Kota stone pricing in Jaipur depends on finish (rough commercial grades, one-side mirror-polished Blue Kota, or calibrated river-wash and shot-blasted non-slip stones), thickness (18mm to 30mm), and lot quantity. Contact Aashiyana Decor on WhatsApp (+91 94686 34597) for wholesale quotes.

What is the difference between Blue Kota and Brown Kota?

Blue Kota (also called greenish-blue) is denser, harder, and features higher calcium content, making it the preferred choice for mirror-polished interior flooring and heavy parking. Brown Kota has higher iron oxide content and is widely used for cost-effective border designs, utility balconies, and pathway pavers.

What thicknesses of Kota stone are available at Aashiyana Decor?

We stock Kota stone in 18mm, 22mm, 25mm, and heavy-duty 30mm thicknesses for commercial parking and external ramps. Sizes range from 2x2 feet, 2x1.5 feet, 2x1 feet, up to large 3x2 and 4x2 feet slabs.

Is Kota stone slippery when wet?

Mirror-polished Kota stone can become smooth when wet. For driveways, car parking, outdoor ramps, and garden steps, we recommend River-Wash (leather texture) or Shot-Blasted Kota stone which guarantees anti-skid friction even during heavy Jaipur monsoons.
