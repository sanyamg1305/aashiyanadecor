Makrana Marble FAQs

What is the price of authentic Makrana marble in Jaipur?

Makrana marble prices in Jaipur range from Rs 120 to 220 per sq.ft. for Makrana Kumari grade, Rs 180 to 380 per sq.ft. for classic Makrana Dungri grade, and Rs 350 to 750 per sq.ft. for selected Makrana Albeta white slabs with fine grey veining. Spotless mandir room slabs range between Rs 450 and 850 per sq.ft.

Why is Makrana marble considered superior for home mandirs and temples?

Makrana marble contains over 98 percent pure calcium carbonate (calcite), making it virtually non-porous compared to other Indian marbles. It does not turn yellow over decades, never requires artificial epoxy resin filling, and stays naturally cool and spiritually pure, which is why it was used to construct the Taj Mahal, Dilwara Temples, and Victoria Memorial.

How to differentiate between Makrana marble and Morwad or Ambaji marble?

Makrana marble features dense micro-crystals that create a deep crystalline translucence and a ring-like metallic sound when tapped. Morwad marble has softer dolomitic grains and greenish or brownish quartz veins, whereas Ambaji white has larger, sugar-like calcite crystals. Aashiyana Decor provides single-lot origin certification for every Makrana block.

Does Makrana marble need chemical sealing?

No. Due to its dense interlocking calcite crystal structure, authentic Makrana marble has very low natural porosity. It polishes to a high mirror shine with traditional tin-oxide powder and water polishing without requiring any artificial surface coating or resin.
