C-Scheme FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can Aashiyana Decor deliver stone slabs to C-Scheme or Civil Lines?

Our Gopalpura Bypass stockyard is located just 15 minutes from C-Scheme via Sahakar Marg or Tonk Road. Stock materials including Specta Quartz slabs, gangsaw granite, and designer tiles are dispatched with same-day or next-day scheduled crane delivery.

What marble is recommended for heritage and luxury villas in Civil Lines?

For heritage estates and luxury renovations in Civil Lines, clients consistently prefer bookmatched Italian Statuario White, Calacatta Gold, and Grey William marble. We provide full dry-lay floor previews so your architect can inspect seamless veining under natural daylight before cutting.

Can Aashiyana Decor supply jumbo quartz slabs for penthouse kitchens in C-Scheme?

Yes. As an authorized Specta Quartz distributor, we stock jumbo slabs (3200x1600mm) in 15mm and 20mm thicknesses with zero-stain warranty against turmeric, oil, and coffee, ideal for seamless waterfall kitchen islands.
